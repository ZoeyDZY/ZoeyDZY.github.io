I am **Zoey Dong** (publishing as **Zoey Zhiyuan Dong**), a PhD student in Physics at the **University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign**, working with [Prof. Nicolás Yunes](https://physics.illinois.edu/people/directory/profile/nyunes). Before joining UIUC, I completed my undergraduate studies in Physics Education at Central China Normal University. My research experience began during an exchange at The Chinese University of Hong Kong and subsequently continued at the University of Virginia.

My research lies at the intersection of **gravitational physics, neutron-star astrophysics, and dense-matter physics**. I am broadly interested in how the macroscopic behavior of compact objects can be used to probe fundamental physics under extreme conditions.

Within this broader context, I am particularly interested in understanding how the **internal physics of neutron stars** can be probed through their **dynamics, oscillations, and gravitational-wave signatures**.
