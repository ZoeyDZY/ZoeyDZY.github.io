I am particularly interested in understanding how the **internal physics of neutron stars** can be probed through their **structure, dynamics, oscillations, and gravitational-wave signatures**.

  <div style="flex: 2 1 420px;">
    <p>
      The properties of matter are relatively well constrained around <strong>nuclear saturation density</strong>, while perturbative QCD provides theoretical guidance at asymptotically high densities. The densities reached in neutron-star cores, however, lie in an intermediate regime where the equation of state remains highly uncertain.
    </p>

    <p>
      The core may contain <strong>nucleonic matter, hyperons, deconfined quark matter, or other exotic phases</strong>, and phase transitions may occur in the stellar interior. Understanding how this uncertain microphysics manifests itself in observable neutron-star properties is one of the central questions motivating my research.
    </p>
